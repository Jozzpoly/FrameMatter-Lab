That trigger is **not met**.

## Why the historical failure is not host evidence

The first P1 Owner candidate contained a catastrophic black/poorly readable presentation failure, but the recovery campaign reproduced a concrete project-side environment/ambient cause and corrected it inside Godot.

Since then, every subsequent visual plane has been improved and defended without requiring a renderer escape hatch:

- G2: balanced Environment/key/fill lighting restored form/depth readability,
- G3: derived exposed-surface cell grid established editable Matter scale,
- G3-S: derived state contour/focus crown established Space state semantics,
- G4: production Camera3D/SpringArm plus geometry-aware composition recovery made the scene usable as an experiment instrument,
- G5: ordinary depth-tested mesh presentation plus pointer acquisition established interaction hierarchy,
- G6: a presentation-only world datum established stable scale/parallax for real motion/topology causality.

These are normal engine facilities, not workarounds around a renderer that fundamentally cannot express the required scene.

## Complexity check

The promoted presentation mechanisms remain small, separable consumers rather than renderer-specific architecture:

- `P1MatterSurfaceGrid`,
- `P1MatterStatePresentation`,
- `P1MatterTargetPresentation`,
- `P1InteractionFeedback`,
- `P1WorldReferencePresentation`,
- the bounded `P1CameraRig` composition policy.

They do not require replacing Matter authority, collision, topology, provider lifecycle or the project architecture to obtain readable pixels.

That matters more than whether another stack could render an attractive equivalent screenshot. G7 asks whether the current host is materially blocking this research instrument. Current evidence says no.

## Decision

**Stay on Godot for P1. Do not build a web reference renderer now.**

A host comparison remains allowed later if new evidence exposes a concrete blocker such as:

- an acceptance-critical visual effect that ordinary Godot facilities cannot express at reasonable complexity,
- a renderer/backend defect that survives a minimal reproduction and project-side falsification,
- unacceptable platform/runtime constraints for an actual target requirement,
- presentation architecture becoming dominated by engine workarounds rather than research needs.

None of those conditions is presently demonstrated.

## What G7 does not claim

G7 does not claim:

- Godot is the permanent engine for all future FrameMatter/Planet Matter work,
- Godot is superior to web/native alternatives in general,
- renderer performance at planetary scale is proven,
- future large-world precision, streaming, compute or custom-rendering requirements are solved,
- current UI hierarchy is acceptable,
- P1 is Owner-ready.

It only closes the current host-switch question at the level justified by evidence.
