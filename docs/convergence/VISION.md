# FrameMatter Convergence — vision and research laws

Status: **active north-star document**.

This document captures the current direction after the P1 experiential regressions, Spark recovery and exact-artifact C0 qualification. It is intentionally more stable than `CURRENT.md` and less implementation-specific than the historical P1 documents.

## North star

FrameMatter is research toward a **living material world**.

The world should not primarily be assembled from predeclared physics-object classes. Instead, geometry, Matter identity, local material/structural laws, state and causal history should determine how the world is physically organized now.

The player should increasingly be able to reason in world language:

> this material is connected this way, supported this way, constrained this way, damaged this way, carrying this load, moving because of that history

rather than engine language:

> this is body A joined to body B by component C.

Engine bodies, providers, colliders, joints and partitions may remain necessary execution structures. They are derived machinery, not automatically the canonical meaning of the world.

## The protected Spark

The first important experiential Spark is:

> ordinary editable WORLD Matter → destructive change alters topology → a portion acquires physical autonomy → the actor can remain materially supported through the transition → Matter moves under physics → the same moving Matter remains editable.

This is valuable because autonomy is a **consequence of the material world**, not a launch mode selected for a special object.

C0 protects this Spark at exact exported-release level.

## Current three-track model

### 1. WORLD LANGUAGE — primary

Discover the smallest language in which local properties/relations can reorganize physical reality without requiring predeclared object categories.

Immediate pressure:

> adjacency must not imply rigid connectivity.

Bearing/rotational freedom is a high-value falsifier because two adjacent regions may belong to one authored material situation while not being one rigid island.

Do not conclude in advance that the answer is `BearingMeaning`, `InterfaceMeaning`, a Godot joint, or a permanent graph schema.

### 2. SCALE / REPRESENTATION — parallel bounded probe

The current 1-cell world may be too coarse for the Owner to naturally think “I can build mechanisms here.”

First answer the perceptual/authoring question cheaply:

> at what player : minimal-Matter ratio does construction start to feel expressive rather than blocky?

Only after that should a real finer-resolution representation campaign be justified.

C1 Owner play now establishes a bounded pressure signal: relative scale is not cosmetic. The Owner found roughly 50–25 cm proxies strongest, while ≈12.5 cm was extreme but still interesting. This does **not** choose a final representation; it shows that future world-language primitives will be experienced differently depending on authoring scale.

### 3. EMBODIMENT / WORK — later multiplier

Longer-term material interaction should increasingly be embodied and causal: carrying, pushing, cutting, supporting, building, damaging, climbing and operating mechanisms should have world-grounded consequences.

Do not start this track merely because it is attractive. Let WORLD LANGUAGE and Owner play expose which forms of work create the most leverage.

C1 also produced the first strong new pressure on this track: at larger body/world ratios the query-based capsule's lack of convincing bidirectional reaction with Matter becomes increasingly obvious, and camera/body framing degrades at the extreme. Preserve this as evidence; do not let it pre-empt C2.

## What this phase is not

Not currently:

- a vehicle framework;
- a catalogue of joints/mechanisms;
- a Minecraft clone;
- a production terrain/streaming architecture;
- a final voxel resolution decision;
- a final data model;
- a new governance campaign;
- a performance campaign without demonstrated pressure;
- an attempt to preserve old P1 composition merely because it was heavily validated.

## Research method

Use a repeating loop:

> recover live truth → name the smallest uncertain claim → build the cheapest discriminating experiment → qualify technical behavior → expose only genuine experiential questions to the Owner → update direction from evidence.

A negative result is useful if it eliminates a wrong direction cheaply.

Generalization must be earned by repeated pressure.
