# FrameMatter Convergence — decision ledger

Purpose: preserve decisions that materially constrain future interpretation without turning hypotheses into permanent architecture.

## D1 — living material world is the active north star

**Decision:** the project is driven by a world whose physical organization emerges from Matter, geometry, local rules and causal history.

**Consequence:** engine object categories are implementation candidates, not automatically world ontology.

## D2 — Spark is protected as experiential evidence

**Decision:** ordinary Matter acquiring autonomy through topology, while retaining identity/support/editability, is a valuable world-law specimen and must remain reproducible.

**Consequence:** later experiments re-run C0 unless deliberately challenging it.

## D3 — WORLD LANGUAGE is primary

**Decision:** the next deep research question is how local material/structural meaning reorganizes physical reality.

**Consequence:** representation and embodiment remain important but subordinate to decision-relevant pressure.

## D4 — scale is measured before rebuilt

**Decision:** use a cheap relative-scale proxy before committing to finer Matter representation.

**Consequence:** C1 can falsify “resolution is the main blocker” without paying for a new storage/render/physics architecture.

## D5 — adjacency != rigid connectivity

**Decision:** adjacency must not be assumed to define one rigid body.

**Consequence:** C2 will challenge the current connected-component→rigid-organization assumption with the smallest local structural law.

## D6 — Bearing is a falsifier, not ontology

**Decision:** a rotational seam/bearing is a high-value first specimen if C2 succeeds.

**Consequence:** do not predesign a universal `BearingMeaning`, `InterfaceMeaning`, joint graph or permanent API.

## D7 — Owner play chooses generalization

**Decision:** technical feasibility does not decide whether a primitive deserves promotion.

**Consequence:** C5/C7 depend on whether play actually expands useful construction possibilities.

## D8 — exact artifact is required when build mode can matter

**Decision:** source/debug green is insufficient for protected release behavior.

**Evidence:** C0 discovered release-only loss of W0 authority mutations hidden in `assert(...)`.

**Consequence:** critical Convergence candidates must qualify the exact exported executable.

## D9 — historical P1/Q0 is evidence, not current steering authority

**Decision:** retain previous quality/recovery work and failures, but do not let stale “Q0 current” documentation redirect current execution.

**Consequence:** `docs/convergence/CURRENT.md` is the active mutable pointer.


## D10 — C7 closes the relation-first priority

**Decision:** C7 is a material experiential FAIL even though C2-C6 remain bounded technical PASSes.

**Consequence:** additional hinge/relation work is frozen. The next work must test physical reciprocity between the actor and Matter.

## D11 — one challenger between Owner gates

**Decision:** after C7, do not chain multiple research questions before the next playable Owner build.

**Consequence:** the current branch may implement and qualify reciprocal contact, including fixes required to make that single challenger truthful, but must not silently expand into representation, large-world, reconnect, or a new player architecture campaign.
